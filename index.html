Zone 4: The Abyss

Remaining in total darkness, abyssal plains contain massive structural ruins safely protected from surface turbulence. Due to freezing conditions and a near-complete lack of wood-boring organisms, historical structures found here present pristine physical data models.

1867

RMS Rhone

An iron-hulled steamship that sank during a severe hurricane in the Caribbean. Because it sits in relatively shallow water (approx 25m), it remains a primary study point for early steam engineering degradation systems.

How do ships sink?

Whether compromised through internal stress patterns, structural impacts, or weather dynamics, vessels go through major hydrodynamic forces during their descent to the seafloor, shifting structural layouts permanently.

How do shipwrecks get rust?

Deep ocean microbes feed directly on iron alloys. Over decades, these biological clusters form unique stalactite-like iron formations known as rusticles, which slowly recycling heavy plating back into oceanic chemical cycles.
